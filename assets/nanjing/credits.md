# Nanjing graduation project images

Images extracted without visual alteration from Feng Qi’s supplied graduation thesis,
*Design and printing of 3D relief shading map in Nanjing* (2026), NUIST Reading Academy.
The author requested these images for this personal portfolio. Original PDF pages 30–43 and 46–47;
figure numbers are listed in the gallery captions. The complete PDF and its administrative pages
are not included in this website.

Data attribution as described in the thesis: NASA SRTM Version 3 (30 m), Copernicus DEM (30 m), Sentinel-2 L2A imagery
(10 m, 2023), © OpenStreetMap contributors, and administrative/base-map sources identified
in the thesis (including Tianditu and the Ministry of Natural Resources standard map).
The gallery preserves the original image labels and credits. Maps are project visualisations;
rendered detail should not be interpreted as measured building-height accuracy.
