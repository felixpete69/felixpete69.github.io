# Qi Feng

Portfolio of GIS, terrain modelling and 3D printing. Live at https://felixpete69.github.io/

## The site

An old computer plays a reel of my work, then the view falls from orbit to a 3D model of Nanjing. Scroll to move through elevation, satellite imagery, the road network and the printed model, and on to London at the same scale. Below that are more projects, work experience and time on stage.

- Click, scroll or press any key to start, or skip the opening.
- Move the pointer to move the sun, and drag to turn the model.
- Select a district name to fly to it.
- The site follows the system's reduced-motion setting and shows a still image where WebGL is unavailable.

`index.html` is self-contained. The CV and the Nanjing case study are the two PDFs beside it.
